## Is Scam With Your Friends out now?
Yes. Steam lists an October 15, 2026 Early Access release and a purchase option as of October 28. Older pages describing a planned release or a playtest refer to an earlier stage.

## How much does it cost?
Check the [official Steam purchase panel](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4954910/Scam_With_Your_Friends/?l=english) while using your own region. Currency, promotions and bundles change; the total shown for a bundle is not necessarily the standalone game's price. This page does not treat a temporary discount as a permanent price.

## Is Early Access the full release?
No. Development is continuing, so features and balance can change. Do not treat a target for the full release as a guaranteed date or assume a wishlist, demo or playtest entitlement includes the paid game.

## Buying a Steam key
Before buying, confirm the exact title, PC platform, activation region and whether the product is a full game or another entitlement. A Steam key does not establish an Xbox or PS5 version, and a low advertised price does not establish seller authorization. Follow the developer's store link to identify the correct game.
