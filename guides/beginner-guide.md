## Start with the current release
Scam With Your Friends entered Steam Early Access on October 15, 2026. It is a fictional call-center game with single-player and online co-op. Broadband internet is required for the online AI conversation systems.

## First-session checklist
These are preparation suggestions, not a playtested winning sequence:

1. Check the [audio setup](/guides/audio-microphone-setup/) and the controls displayed in your build.
2. Read the current quota and available tasks. Do not assume an older guide's fixed first-day target.
3. Follow the in-game prompts and check which completed actions count toward the objective.
4. In co-op, agree who speaks and who records the shared goal; use the [online play guide](/guides/online-play-platforms/) for dependencies and platform questions.
5. Read the boss review before planning another run. The [shift planner](/tools/shift-planner/) records your own inputs; it does not predict hidden game rules.

## If you get stuck
Record the game build, objective and visible error. Separate an audio problem from a lobby problem or an AI conversation failure. Check official announcements before resetting progress. Exact payout values, trust thresholds and guaranteed scripts have not been established here.
