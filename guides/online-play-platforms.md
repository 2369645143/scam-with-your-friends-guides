## Can you play online with friends?
Yes. The current Steam page lists online co-op, public lobbies and single-player. The game entered Early Access on October 15, 2026. Single-player does not establish offline AI conversations. In standard builds, 0.1.95 removes optional OpenRouter and Gladia integrations and reverts old OpenRouter selections to Official Backend; see [speech settings](/guides/audio-microphone-setup/).

## Is it on Xbox or PS5?
The checked sources establish the Windows Steam release. They do not provide a confirmed Xbox or PS5 release date. Controller input on PC and a console release are different features; do not buy a PC key expecting it to activate on a console.

## Is online play free in a browser?
The official Steam listing sells a downloadable PC game. The word “online” describes its networked play; the cited sources do not establish an official free browser version. Start from the developer's Steam link rather than a similarly named download page.

## Prepare a co-op session
1. Have everyone check the same game and available update in Steam.
2. Check microphone input and game audio before starting an AI call.
3. Use the lobby and join options visible in your current build. Public lobbies are listed, but this page does not claim an unverified invitation shortcut or player cap.
4. If joining or dialogue fails, note whether it affects one player or the whole group. Check official announcements and report the build, error and reproduction steps.
5. Agree who is speaking and who is tracking the shared objective. The [shift planner](/tools/shift-planner/) can keep your own quota notes.

These are preparation suggestions, not tested fixes. Do not change firewall rules or delete saves based only on an unspecified connection error.
