## Voice Input: The Core of the Game

*Scam With Your Friends* is built around live conversations with dynamic, unpredictable AI callers. While text typing is supported, using a functional microphone is by far the fastest and most immersive way to persuade callers and hit your shift quotas.

---

## 1. Microphone Configuration & Best Practices

1. **Default Input Device**: Set your primary gaming headset as the default communication device in Windows/Mac settings before launching Steam.
2. **Push-to-Talk vs Open Mic**:
   * **Push-to-Talk (Recommended)**: Prevents background fan noise, mechanical keyboard clicks, and teammate banter from leaking into the AI caller's ear.
   * **Open Mic**: Best if you are playing solo or in a quiet room, but risks triggering unintended AI responses.
3. **Headphones are Mandatory**: Never use external speakers. Sound looping into the mic causes feedback loops and confuses the AI's transcription engine.

---

## 2. How Office Proximity Voice Chat Works

The game features dynamic 3D proximity audio across the call center floor:
* **Coworker Overhearing**: Teammates sitting at adjacent desks can physically hear your conversations with clients.
* **Chaotic Interference**: If your coworker screams, throws objects, or sets off a fire extinguisher next to your desk, the AI caller on your line may react with suspicion or ask what the loud noise was.
* **Co-op Assist**: A nearby teammate can whisper suggested fake scenarios or prompts for you to say out loud to trick suspicious callers.

---

## 3. Can You Play with Text-Only Input?

Yes. If you do not have a working microphone or prefer not to speak:
* You can interact with the caller via on-screen text typing at your terminal.
* **Trade-off**: Text input takes longer to compose during rapid 20-minute shifts, but guarantees clear phrasing without microphone distortion or accent misinterpretations.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting AI Response Delays & OpenRouter

The game utilizes an **OpenRouter cloud connection** for real-time generative dialogue:
* **Normal Latency**: Expect approximately 1.5 to 3 seconds between finishing your sentence and the caller responding.
* **If Callers Stop Responding**:
  1. Check if broadband internet remains stable.
  2. Inspect the in-game terminal for connection status alerts.
  3. Verify that your microphone sensitivity threshold is registering your voice clearly.
